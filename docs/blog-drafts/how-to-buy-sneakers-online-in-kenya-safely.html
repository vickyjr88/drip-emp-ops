Buying sneakers online is convenient, especially if you can't get into town. It's also where most bad experiences happen: the wrong size, a pair that isn't what the photos showed, or money sent to someone who then disappears. A few habits remove most of that risk.

1. Check the seller is real

Before you order, look for signs that there's a real business behind the page:

  • a physical address you could actually visit, and opening hours;
  • a phone or WhatsApp number that gets answered, and the same details everywhere (website, Instagram, Google);
  • photos that are clearly the seller's own, not stock images or pictures copied from elsewhere;
  • written policies for delivery and exchanges that you can read before paying.

If the only way to reach a seller is a number that never replies, that tells you what you need to know.

2. Get your size right

Sizing is the most common reason for returns. Many pairs are sold in EUR sizes, but fit varies by brand and model, and some run small or large. Measure your foot, read our sneaker size guide, and ask the seller if the model fits true to size. Also ask whether your size is on the shelf or has to be sourced, and how long that takes. An honest seller will tell you.

3. Ask to see the actual pair

A quick photo or video of the exact pair, in your size, sent on WhatsApp, costs the seller a minute and saves you a lot of doubt. Look at the box label, the size tag inside the tongue, the stitching and the sole. If a seller won't show you, think twice. For what to look for, see how to spot fake sneakers.

4. Know the total cost before you pay

The shoe price is only part of it. Ask what delivery will cost and how long it will take to reach you, before you pay anything. Delivery cost depends on where the parcel is going, so a seller who quotes a delivery price without asking where you are may be leaving something out. At Drip Emporium delivery isn't added at checkout for exactly this reason: we call you after you order to confirm the cost. More on our delivery page.

5. Pay in a way that protects you

  • Use a proper checkout where you can. A card or M-Pesa checkout through an established payment provider is safer than sending money to an individual's number.
  • Check the name shown on your M-Pesa prompt matches the business you're buying from.
  • Be careful with deposits. Being asked to send money to a personal number for an order you've never seen anything from is a classic warning sign.
  • Never share your M-Pesa PIN or a one-time code, for any reason, with anyone.
  • Prefer pay on collection if you're able to pick up from the shop.
  • Keep the payment confirmation message and any chat history.

6. Inspect the pair when it arrives

Check the parcel while the courier is still there if you can, or as soon as you get it. Look for:

  • the right model and colour, and the size you ordered;
  • both shoes matching, with no visible damage or marks;
  • the box and any tags intact.

Take a short video as you open the box. If something is wrong, contact the seller the same day with photos. Don't wear the shoes outside until you're happy with them: unworn pairs in their original box are far easier to exchange.

7. Ask about exchanges before you order

A good seller will tell you how exchanges work before you pay. At Drip Emporium you can message us on WhatsApp as soon as you can if a pair isn't right, and unworn shoes in their original box are the easiest to swap. We'd rather sort out a wrong size than leave you with shoes you won't wear.

Warning signs, in short

  • A price far below every other seller's for the same model.
  • No address, no landline, and a number that doesn't answer.
  • Pressure to pay quickly, or to pay into a personal number.
  • No clear answer on delivery cost or exchanges.
  • A seller who won't send a photo of the actual pair.

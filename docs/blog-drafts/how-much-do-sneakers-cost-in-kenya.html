What makes one pair cost more than another

  • The model and its demand. Pairs people are chasing cost more than long-running staples.
  • Special editions. Custom colourways and collaborations carry a premium over the standard colours of the same shoe.
  • Materials and build. Leather, suede and layered, technical uppers cost more to make than simple canvas or mesh.
  • Whether it's on the shelf. A pair that has to be sourced for you in your size can differ in price from one that's in stock.
  • The seller. A shop with a real address, real stock and clear policies has costs a pop-up seller doesn't.

Why the cheapest pair isn't always the deal

If a price is far below what everyone else charges for the same model, ask why before you celebrate. Counterfeits are a real risk in Kenya, and an unusually low price is the first thing to question. Read how to spot fake sneakers and, wherever you buy, try the pair on and inspect it before you pay.

How to compare prices fairly

  1. Same model, same colourway, same size. Compare exactly the same pair.
  2. Add delivery. The total is what counts. We confirm delivery cost with you after you order, since it depends on where the parcel is going (see our delivery page).
  3. Ask about exchanges. A slightly cheaper pair you can't swap if it doesn't fit isn't a saving.
  4. Check you can inspect it. The ability to try before you pay is worth something.
